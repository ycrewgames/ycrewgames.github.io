BreachNight Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · App: BreachNight (com.ycrewgames.breachnight) · Developer: YCrew Games, a brand of Dust In Peace LLC

This policy explains what information BreachNight collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. BreachNight is intended for players aged 13 and older.

Information we collect

WhatWhy
Game progress: cash, gold, weapons, upgrades, unlocked levels, daily tasks, settings and languageTo run the game. Progress is saved on your phone and protected against tampering.
Google Play Games sign-in (optional): your Play Games player ID and display nameTo back up your progress to your own Google Play Games Saved Games, restore it on another device, and let you switch accounts
Purchase records (if in-app purchases are offered): product, Google Play / App Store order IDTo deliver gold and make sure each order is paid out only once
Advertising ID, device information and ad interaction data, collected by our ad partnersTo show ads (including optional rewarded ads), cap how often you see them, measure them and, where you allow it, personalize them

BreachNight does not run its own game server and does not ask you to type a name or email. We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise GPS location, or microphone/camera data. Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device; we do not send them from a server.

Third-party services

BreachNight uses these services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity (game engine, LevelPlay ad mediation, Unity Ads) – Unity privacy policy
  • Google (Google Play Games sign-in and Saved Games, Google Play Billing, AdMob ads) – Google privacy policy, how Google uses data from partner apps
  • Other ad networks connected through LevelPlay mediation, which may receive the advertising ID and device information to bid on and show ads
  • Apple (App Store, Game Center) on iPhone – Apple privacy policy

We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • Terms and privacy: the game asks you to accept these on first launch. Ads only start after you accept.
  • Ad personalization: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, or turn off ad personalization there. On iPhone, the app asks permission before tracking.
  • Notifications: the game asks once whether you want reminders. You can turn them off any time in Settings (gear icon, top right) › NOTIFICATIONS, or in your phone settings.
  • Sign-in: playing as a guest is fine. Signing in with Google Play Games is optional, and you can switch accounts in Settings.
  • Delete your data: Settings › DELETE ACCOUNT in the game, or see how to delete your BreachNight data.

How long we keep data

Progress stays on your phone until you delete it or uninstall the game. Cloud backups stay in your Google Play Games Saved Games until you delete your account in the game or remove the game's data in Google Play Games. Purchase order records are kept for fraud prevention and accounting as the law requires.

Children

BreachNight is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will help remove the data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and to object to or limit certain processing. Email us to make a request; we respond within 30 days.

Changes

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant, the game will ask you to accept them again. The date at the top shows the latest version.
