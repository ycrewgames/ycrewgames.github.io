Loudhead Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026 · App: Loudhead (com.ycrewvision.loudhead) · Developer: YCrew Games, a brand of Dust In Peace LLC

This policy explains what information Loudhead collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Loudhead is a horror game intended for players aged 13 and older.

Information we collect

Loudhead has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases. Your settings (such as the graphics level) are stored only on your phone.

WhatWhy
Advertising ID and ad interaction dataTo show an ad when you die in the game, cap how often you see ads, measure them, and (if you allow it) personalize them
Device data: device model, OS version, language, app version, approximate location (country/region from IP address)Needed by our ad partners to deliver ads that work on your device and to prevent ad fraud
Your consent choicesTo remember whether you agreed to personalized ads

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise GPS location, or microphone/camera data.

Third-party services

Loudhead uses these services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity (Unity Ads and LevelPlay ad mediation) – Unity privacy policy
  • Google (AdMob ads, User Messaging Platform consent) – Google privacy policy, how Google uses data from partner apps

We do not sell your personal information. Ad partners may use the advertising ID to show personalized ads only where you have allowed it.

Your choices

  • Ad personalization: in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where it applies, a consent form asks you on first launch. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your phone. For ad data held by our partners, email us and we will help you make a request.

Children

Loudhead is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and to object to or limit certain processing. Email us to make a request; we respond within 30 days.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
