AI, Make This Game For Me! Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026 · App: AI, Make This Game For Me! (com.ycrewgames.aimakethisgame) · Developer: YCrew Games, a brand of Dust In Peace LLC

This policy explains what information AI, Make This Game For Me! collects and the choices you have. The game is a paid, offline life and game-studio simulator intended for players aged 13 and older.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. It does not send any personal information to us or to third parties.

Your saves and settings (such as graphics level, audio volume and language) are stored only on your device. The in-game "AI" tools, chats and prompts are part of the simulation: they run entirely inside the game and are not sent to any AI service.

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, location, advertising ID, or microphone/camera data.

Purchases

You buy the game through Google Play. Google processes the payment under the Google privacy policy; we only receive standard, non-identifying sales reports from Google Play.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, removes all saves and settings.
  • Backups: if your phone's backup service (for example Android backup) copies app data, that copy is handled by your device provider under their policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access or delete personal data. Because the game collects none, there is normally nothing for us to provide, but you can email us with any question and we respond within 30 days.

Changes

If a future update adds online features, we will update this policy before they ship. The date at the top shows the latest version.
