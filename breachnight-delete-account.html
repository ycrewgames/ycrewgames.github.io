Delete your BreachNight account and data

App: BreachNight (com.ycrewgames.breachnight) · Developer: YCrew Games, a brand of Dust In Peace LLC

In the game (fastest)

Tap the gear icon (top right of the main menu) › DELETE ACCOUNT and confirm. Your progress is erased from your phone, your cloud backup in Google Play Games is deleted, and the game starts fresh.

Without the app

If you signed in with Google Play Games, you can remove BreachNight's saved data from your Google Play Games settings. You can also email us from the Google account you used and we will help, replying within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • All saved progress: cash, gold, weapons, upgrades, unlocked levels, daily tasks and settings
  • Your cloud backup in Google Play Games Saved Games, and the game's link to your Play Games account

What may be kept

  • Purchase order records (order ID, product, date), kept for fraud prevention and tax/accounting for as long as the law requires
  • Data held by ad partners under their own policies. Reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings to disconnect it.

Google Play and App Store purchase history is held by Google/Apple, not by us. Deleting your data does not refund purchases.
